# NL Data Notes

## Methodology & Conventions

Data scope: National League only, 1876-2025. Win percentages rounded to 3 decimal places. Schedule lengths: Pre-1892 varied; 1892-1960=154 games; 1961-1989=162; 1994=cancelled; 2020=60.

### Key Caveats
1. Franchise totals are CUMULATIVE (Brooklyn/LA Dodgers includes NY era)
2. 1994 season voided (no WS or pennant)
3. Pre-1903 pennants = NL championship only
4. Milwaukee Brewers switched from AL to NL Central in 1998
5. Montreal Expos relocated to Washington Nationals in 2005

### Era Definitions
- Founding Era (1876-1892): 8-team, smallest schedules
- Dead Ball Era (1893-1919): Low-scoring, strategic play
- Live Ball Era (1920-1940): Power surge
- WWII Era (1941-1945): War-affected
- Post-War (1946-1960): Migration begins
- Expansion Era (1961-1969): 162 games, divisions
- Modern Era (2006-present): Advanced analytics
